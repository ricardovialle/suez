suez · response-eQTL mapping in R

eQTLs and response eQTLs in one mixed model

suez tests every cis-SNP for an effect on expression and for an effect that changes with a condition: a treatment, a time point, a dose or age. It fits all samples at once, with repeated samples of an individual, relatedness and hidden confounders in one random effect, so the tests stay calibrated where a plain regression is not.

y = X0b + β·G + (G × C)·δ + e,   e ~ N(0, σ²kK + σ²I)

X0: condition terms and covariates. G: the SNP's ALT allele count. C: condition columns (a factor's levels, a number, or a spline basis). K: the samples × samples covariance learned in step 1. The eQTL test compares the models with and without β; the response test compares them with and without δ.

Two steps

How a run works

Step 1 · learn_covariance()

One sample covariance for all genes

Fits latent factors (hidden confounders), a same-individual term and optional kinship from all expressed genes. Run time depends on the number of samples, not genes.

Step 2 · map_interaction_qtl()

Nested mixed models per SNP

For each gene and cis-SNP: no-genotype, genotype, and genotype × condition models with exact maximum likelihood; likelihood-ratio tests p_geno and p_interact.

Optional

Bootstrap, local ancestry, curves

A parametric bootstrap draw per SNP for calibration, ancestry-specific effects in admixed samples, and β(condition) curves for continuous conditions.
